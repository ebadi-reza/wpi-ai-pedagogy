How I Use AI in My Teaching Workflow

Reza Ebadi, PhD
Assistant Professor of Teaching, Mechanical & Materials Engineering
AI Pedagogy Specialist, Morgan Teaching and Learning Center

Projects vs Chats Course Design Faculty Workflow 15 min

A walkthrough of how I use AI Projects (in Claude and ChatGPT) instead of one-off chats to keep context, isolate memory, and organize work by theme.
I demo two projects of my own: one that maps every objective in my ME3310 Kinematics syllabus to specific homework, quiz, and exam questions; surfacing lecture topics I never actually assess;
and one that ingests my CV to spot trends in my course evaluations, organize my MQP advising by theme, and draft bios at different lengths.
I also share the "Claude Sandbox" folder convention I use to give AI safe access to local files without exposing sensitive material.

What you can try: Create a new Project in Claude or ChatGPT, upload your CV, and ask it to summarize the trend in your course evaluations by course and flag anything worth a second look.
