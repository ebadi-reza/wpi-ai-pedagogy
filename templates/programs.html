Programs & Initiatives

Explore our ongoing programs designed to support faculty in thoughtfully integrating AI into teaching. From hands-on labs to collaborative sessions, we create spaces for experimentation, learning, and community building.

AI Pedagogy Labs

Monthly interactive sessions where faculty experiment with AI tools and explore pedagogical strategies in a supportive, judgment-free environment. Each lab focuses on a specific theme relevant to teaching and learning.

Lab 2: Assignment and Assessment Redesign

The second pedagogy lab will be an interactive session exploring effective (and ineffective) ways to integrate AI into course assignments and assessments. Bring one of your own assignments, and together we’ll workshop strategies to thoughtfully redesign it for the AI era.

Lab 1: Ethics and Academic Integrity

The first AI Pedagogy Lab, held on October 9, 2025, brought together 17 WPI faculty and staff and 1 student to explore AI ethics and academic integrity. Through small-group discussions, participants explored ethical questions around AI in teaching and learning across five discussion themes. Explore findings by theme:

Learning & Assessment, Equity & Access, Transparency & Responsibility, Privacy & Data Use, and Sustainability.
